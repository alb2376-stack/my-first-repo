Scaffolding Survey

Scaffolding and sidewalk sheds are a familiar part of New York City, protecting pedestrians while buildings undergo maintenance. Although they serve an important safety function, they often affect how people experience streets and public spaces. We are conducting this survey to better understand how the public perceives these structures. Your feedback will help us explore new ideas for scaffolding that improve safety, comfort, accessibility, and the overall quality of the streetscape.
