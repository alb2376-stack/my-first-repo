Geospatial Structures

Mapping location-based data. Continuing the exploration and research I've been doing in both Mario, Luc and Meli's class, I used the same sidewalk shed data to create an interactive map to visualize the distribution of these structures across the city. I used mapbox-gl.js for this implementation.

Mapping Active Sidewalk Sheds in NYC.

Extension: Mapping Systems Final Project

I carried this further into a final project for Mapping Systems with Mario, working from the same CSV data. There I looked at what sidewalk sheds imply about municipal deficiencies, and at the patterns and reinforcement that show up across community districts. Rather than counting sheds alone, I explored mapping averages, and also I measured what percentage of a district's sidewalk they actually cover, and closed with a speculative cost map.
