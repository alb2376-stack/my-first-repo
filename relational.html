Relational Structures

An extension of the precedent study from Colloquium I, asking how classifying something can help you understand the system it belongs to. Working from a journal article on e-waste that I came across, I created a classification system for e-waste, and visualized it in a way that makes the system legible, and one thing is hard to miss. Almost every path eventually ends at landfill. This exercise was a great way to understand networks and visualize them in a way that is legible and easy to understand.
